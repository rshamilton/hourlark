Quick help

How do I get hours signed off?

On a cause set to "Needs to be signed off", open Sign-offs, create a verification sheet PDF, print it, get it signed, then scan or photograph it back in. Entries on a signed sheet are locked.

How do I export my hours?

Open Reports, choose a period and cause, then export PDF, Excel (.xlsx) or CSV.

Where are my backups?

In the Files app under On My iPhone, then Hourlark, then Backups. Restore them from Settings, Backup & Files, or from the welcome screen on a fresh install.

The widget or Stop button doesn't work.

Those need App Groups. If you built the app yourself with a free developer account, they are unavailable; see the README.
