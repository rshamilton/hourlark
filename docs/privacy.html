Privacy policy

Hourlark does not collect or share any data. Your hours stay on your device, with an automatic backup to your own iCloud Drive that you can turn off.

What Hourlark collects

Nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no third-party code. The app contains no networking code and we run no servers, so it cannot send your information to us. The one exception to "stays on your device" is the iCloud Drive backup described below, which goes to your own iCloud account, not to us.

What is stored, and where

  • Your causes, time entries, notes, sign-off sheets and proof photos are stored on your iPhone.
  • Backup files (JSON, CSV and daily snapshots) are written to the Hourlark folder in the Files app on your iPhone.
  • On by default: if you are signed in to iCloud, iOS copies those backup files to Hourlark's folder in your own iCloud Drive. You can turn this off in Settings, Backup & Files, and you can also pick another folder in Files. That copy is made by Apple under your Apple Account and Apple's privacy policy. We never see it.
  • A small amount of widget data (today's hours, the running timer) is shared between the app and its widgets through an App Group on your device.

Permissions

  • Camera: only to scan a signed sheet. Photos stay on your iPhone.
  • Notifications: only if you turn on reminders. They are scheduled locally on your device.

Sharing

When you export a report or share a file, you choose where it goes using the standard iOS share sheet. Hourlark does not send it anywhere on its own.

Children

Hourlark collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Use Settings, then Erase all data, or delete the app. Backup files in the Files app or iCloud Drive remain until you delete them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the change will appear on this page and in the public source history. Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
